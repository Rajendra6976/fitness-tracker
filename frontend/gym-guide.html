Beginner Gym Guide

Getting Started: Tips for Men & Women

  • Consult a Doctor: If you have any health concerns, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Start Slow: Begin with 2-3 days per week, focusing on full-body workouts.
  • Warm Up: Always start with 5-10 minutes of light cardio and dynamic stretching.
  • Learn Proper Form: Use light weights and focus on technique before increasing load.
  • Balance: Include both strength and cardio exercises for overall fitness.
  • Rest: Allow at least one rest day between sessions for recovery.
  • Hydrate & Eat Well: Drink water and eat a balanced diet to fuel your workouts.

Tips for Women

  • Strength training will not make you bulky; it helps build lean muscle and burn fat.
  • Focus on compound movements (squats, presses, rows) for best results.
  • Don't be afraid to use weights—start light and progress gradually.

Tips for Men

  • Don't skip leg day—train all major muscle groups for balance.
  • Be patient with progress; consistency is key.
  • Stretch after workouts to improve flexibility and reduce soreness.

Remember: Progress takes time. Stay consistent, listen to your body, and enjoy your fitness journey!
