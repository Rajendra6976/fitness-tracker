Help & Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add a workout? Use the form on the Home page to log your exercise. Enter the type, duration, calories, and date, then click "Assign Task".
  • How do I mark a workout as complete? Click the ✅ icon next to the workout in your list to mark it as finished.
  • Where can I see my progress? Go to the Progress page for charts and stats, or the Reports page for detailed tables.
  • How do I set or edit my exercise reminder? Visit the Reminder page to customize your daily exercise message.
  • How do I log out? Use the Logout button at the bottom of the sidebar or in the Settings page.
  • Need more help? Contact support or check the About page for more info.

Fitness Tracker is designed to be intuitive, but if you have questions or feedback, we're here to help you succeed!
